Each part of the story has its own built-in music. To use your own song for a part,
put an mp3 here with that name (lowercase):

  dream.mp3     - intro, lights, first photo
  birthday.mp3  - cake, scratch cards, gift
  tender.mp3    - letter, second photo
  memories.mp3  - photo wall, third photo, floating memories
  party.mp3     - Happy Birthday finale, the question
  vow.mp3       - the promise
  credits.mp3   - thank-you end credits

  voice.mp3     - optional voice note button after the letter

Any file you leave out just uses the built-in tune.
